Advertising disclosure

UK Review Desk publishes editorial pages about gambling brands. Some links labelled Visit are commercial affiliate links. If you follow one, open an account and meet conditions set by the operator, that operator may pay this desk a commission.

What that changes

It should not change the price you pay. It does not change the editorial score, the order of the two reviews, or the drawbacks written on the page. A brand cannot buy a higher mark.

How to spot a commercial link

Visit buttons carry an “Ad” line: 18+, terms apply, BeGambleAware. Register links to gamblingcommission.gov.uk are not affiliate links. Help links to BeGambleAware, GamStop and Gamblers Anonymous are not affiliate links.

Where the buttons go today

Until you replace them with your own tracked URLs, Visit Betano opens betano.co.uk and Visit Ejjabet opens ejjabet.com — the domains named on the public register. Swap those addresses for your affiliate links before you rely on commission.

Editorial rule

If a commercial relationship ever existed that a reader would reasonably want to know about, it belongs on this page. Absence of a named deal means the buttons are still marked as commercial so the possibility is not hidden.
