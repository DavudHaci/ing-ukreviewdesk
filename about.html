About the desk

UK Review Desk is an editorial desk started in 2026. The site publishes reviews of casino brands that face Great Britain. It is not a casino, it does not take deposits, and nothing on these pages can be wagered.

What gets covered

Only brands that can be found on the UK Gambling Commission public register. At the moment that list is Betano and Ejjabet. Unlicensed sites are out of scope, even if they advertise to UK readers.

What a score means

A score is a weighted editorial reading of six public criteria: licensing clarity, payments information, catalogue, support information, safer-gambling tools and mobile presentation. It is not a claim that a reviewer timed a series of withdrawals. Where this desk has not measured something, the page says so.

Commercial links

Visit buttons may be affiliate links. A commission never buys a rank. The rule is written on the disclosure page and repeated beside the buttons.

Independence

Operators do not approve drafts. If a sentence is wrong, the correction belongs on the page, with a date. Write via the contact form or hello@ukreviewdesk.co.uk.
