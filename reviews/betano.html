Licence

On 27 October 2026 the Commission’s domain list for account 39576 showed betano.co.uk as a white-label name of BV Gaming Limited. The same list includes other trading names, which is normal for a white-label holder. White label is not the same phrase as “active” on a holder’s own domain. Open the register entry and read the status that is live on the day you sign up.

The holder’s head office on that page is in Gibraltar. A Gibraltar office does not, by itself, say anything about whether the GB permission is in force. The public register is the document that does.

Banking, as published rather than timed

This page does not claim a measured hour-count for withdrawals. Those figures go stale, and inventing them would mislead you. Before you deposit, read the cashier for the minimum, the methods offered in GBP, and the stated time for cards versus e-wallets. Identity checks are a normal part of GB onboarding; have documents ready so a cash-out is not the moment you discover they are missing.

Lobby

Betano’s public positioning is a sportsbook with casino attached: slots, live roulette and blackjack, and fixed-odds sport. If you only want a narrow slots site, a sportsbook-led lobby can feel larger than you need. If you want sport and tables on one balance, that breadth is the point of the brand.

Safer gambling

Set a deposit limit before the first stake. GB-licensed remote operators must provide limit tools, reality checks and self-exclusion, and they participate in GamStop. Test the path yourself: if a limit takes more than a couple of taps, or a cool-off does not start immediately, treat that as a reason to stop, not as a quirk.

Support

Help is typically offered by live chat and email. This desk has not published a queue-time study. Weekend evenings are when queues usually lengthen across the industry, so do not assume a weekday answer time still applies on a Saturday night.

Verdict

Betano earns the higher editorial mark on this desk because the register trail is easy to follow and the product on offer is broad: sport, slots and live tables under one GB-facing domain. The mark is capped because the domain is a white label, because payout speed is not something this page pretends to have timed, and because promotional terms still need a careful read.

18+ only. Decide a deposit cap and a session length before you pay in anything. If you want to block GB-licensed sites in one step, use GamStop rather than a single brand’s self-exclusion.
