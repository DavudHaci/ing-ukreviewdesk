Privacy

This note describes the preview build of UK Review Desk. Update it before you publish if you add analytics, a hosted contact form, or a company that acts as controller.

What this build stores

A preference flag in local storage under the key ukreviewdesk-consent, and only after you choose Accept, Reject or reopen the banner. It records that choice so the banner can stay closed. It is not sent to a server by this build.

What this build does not do

The contact form does not transmit your message. No analytics tag is loaded. No advertising profile is built. Visit links leave this site for the operator; that site has its own privacy notice, which you should read before you register.

Email

If you email hello@ukreviewdesk.co.uk, the message will contain whatever you put in it. Do not include passwords, card numbers or copies of identity documents unless the desk has specifically asked for a correction that requires them — and even then, prefer describing the issue.

Your choices

You can clear the preference by deleting site data for this domain, or by using Cookie preferences in the footer. To ask about personal data once a real controller is named, use the contact page.
