How we work

How a review page is put together

Six checks. A brand is not offered a drafting role, and a position on this desk is not for sale.

Stage 01

Register lookup

The domain is searched on the UK Gambling Commission public register. Account number, holder and domain status are copied onto the page. No register entry, no review.

Stage 02

Who the customer actually contracts with

White-label domains are labelled as such. The page names the licence holder, not only the brand on the logo.

Stage 03

Published cashier and terms

Deposit minimums, stated payout windows and bonus rules are taken from the operator’s own pages when they are public. This desk does not invent stopwatch times.

Stage 04

Product shape

Sportsbook, slots, live tables or bingo — the page describes the lobby the brand actually advertises, including what is thin.

Stage 05

Safer-gambling tools

Limits, time-outs, self-exclusion and GamStop participation are scored. Friction on the way out costs marks. GB remote operators are expected to offer these tools.

Stage 06

Date the page

Every review shows when the register was last read. If a later check disagrees with the page, the page should be corrected in public.

Editorial rules

What this desk will and will not do

Scores stay editorial

Commercial links are disclosed on every page. Paying for a higher score is outside the rules of this desk.

Register links, not badge images

A licence logo can be pasted onto any site. These pages link to the Commission’s register and print the account number.

Drawbacks stay visible

White-label structure, wagering rules and slower card payouts are written down, not smoothed out of the verdict.

Dates on claims

The register snapshot is dated. If you need today’s status, open the register link rather than trusting an old line.

Safer gambling is scored

Limit tools and the route to GamStop carry real weight. A brand that makes leaving harder should score lower.

Corrections

If an account number or domain flag is wrong, write to the desk and the page should say what changed. Contact.
